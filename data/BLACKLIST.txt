# Alexa's List - NO FOODS
# AIP-style why: avoid grains, dairy, legumes, nightshades, and highly processed inputs.

# GLUTEN & GRAINS
Amaranth
Barley
Buckwheat
Bulgur
Corn
Millet
Oat: limited/moderation only.
Quinoa
Rice: white rice only in moderation.
Rye
Sorghum
Spelt
Wheat

# DAIRY
Butter
Cheese
Cream
Ghee: limited/moderation only.
Milk: use almond milk instead.
Yogurt: use coconut milk yogurt instead.

# LEGUMES
Black beans
Chickpeas: sometimes tolerated if very well processed (e.g., hummus).
Fava beans
Kidney beans
Lentils
Lima beans
Peanuts: limited/moderation only.
Soybeans: use coconut aminos instead of soy sauce.

# NIGHTSHADE VEGETABLES
Eggplant
Goji berries
Ground cherries: not the same as regular cherries.
Peppers: all peppers (bell + spicy).
Red spices: including chili powder and paprika.
Potato
Tomato
Tomatillo

# OTHER
Alcohol
Processed foods
Refined sugars: limit.
Artificial sweeteners: limit; prefer coconut sugar or maple syrup.
