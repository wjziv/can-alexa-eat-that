# Alexa's List - NO FOODS

# GLUTEN & GRAINS
Amaranth: avoid amaranth grains.
Barley: avoid barley.
Buckwheat: avoid buckwheat.
Bulgur: avoid bulgur (listed as bulger in source list).
Corn: avoid corn.
Millet: avoid millet.
Oat: only in moderation; mostly oat milk.
Quinoa: avoid quinoa.
Rice: white rice only in moderation.
Rye: avoid rye.
Sorghum: avoid sorghum.
Spelt: avoid spelt.
Wheat: avoid wheat.

# DAIRY
Butter: avoid butter.
Cheese: avoid cheese.
Cream: avoid cream.
Ghee: only in moderation.
Milk: avoid milk; substitute almond milk.
Yogurt: avoid dairy yogurt; substitute coconut milk yogurt.

# LEGUMES
Black beans: avoid black beans.
Chickpeas: avoid unless very well processed; hummus may be tolerated.
Fava beans: avoid fava beans.
Kidney beans: avoid kidney beans.
Lentils: avoid lentils.
Lima beans: avoid lima beans.
Peanuts: only in moderation.
Soybeans: avoid soybeans; use coconut aminos instead of soy sauce.

# NIGHTSHADE VEGETABLES
Eggplant: avoid eggplant.
Goji berries: avoid goji berries.
Ground cherries: avoid ground cherries (regular cherries are separate).
Peppers: avoid all peppers, including bell and spicy peppers.
Red spices: avoid red spices, including chili powder and paprika.
Potato: avoid potato.
Tomato: avoid tomato.
Tomatillo: avoid tomatillo.

# OTHER
Alcohol: no alcohol.
Processed foods: avoid processed foods.
Refined sugars: limit refined sugars.
Artificial sweeteners: limit artificial sweeteners; use coconut sugar or maple syrup.
